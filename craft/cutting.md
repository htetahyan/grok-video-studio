# Cutting

- **Watch everything once before you cut.** Log usable moments in `shot-log.md` with in/out points. Choosing from notes beats scrubbing.
- **Find the spine first.** For talking footage, that's the sentence that carries the brief. For b-roll pieces, it's the one shot that has to be there.
- **Cut on action or breath.** Enter a shot slightly after motion starts and leave before it settles.
  For dialogue, cut in the breath, never mid-word, and keep about 2–4 frames of air.
- **Story reels (9:16):** hook within 1.5 s, one idea per 2–4 s, end on a clear beat rather than a fade to nothing.
  Assume sound is off for the first second, so captions or on-screen text have to carry the hook.
- **Brand films:** let shots breathe longer (3–6 s). Music structure sets the edit grid; cut on bar lines unless the story needs otherwise.
- **Jump cuts** are a choice. If you use them, use them consistently, and record the choice in `decisions.md`.
- **J- and L-cuts** (sound leads or trails picture) make real footage feel edited by a person rather than assembled.
